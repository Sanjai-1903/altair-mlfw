# Weekly Review  (run every week - project lead)

Date: ______    Attendees: ______

Ask these five questions every single week:

1. Is there new evidence of data leakage, label uncertainty, or a
   spurious terrain-vs-fault shortcut?

2. Did a simpler baseline match or exceed the SNN under the same input
   and false-alarm conditions?

3. Which numeric or hardware constraints changed, and what is their
   measured consequence?

4. Are the data, model and firmware artifacts reproducible by someone
   other than their author?

5. Which specific missing fact is blocking RTL from being frozen?
